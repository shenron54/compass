Rule types

allocation_band: measure is position_weight or group_weight (with symbols), lookthrough_tech or cash_weight, plus min and/or max as fractions (0.65 = 65%).

valuation_freeze: fires when series reaches above and stays on until it falls to resume_below. Series such as CAPE_PCT, NDX_ABOVE_200 or FRED:VIXCLS.

drawdown_ladder: tranches of drawdown (0.10 = -10% from the high), fraction of the budget and an optional buy. The budget is budget if set, otherwise cash above cash_floor. The cycle resets when the price is back within reset_within of its high.

trim_at_high: fires when symbol's price series is above_200dma (0.25 = 25%) over its 200-day average and the weight is at least overweight_by above target (defaults to the portfolio target).

Every rule needs id (lowercase, dashes), name and type. Add a note to remind yourself why it exists.

Series keys: FRED:<id>, MULTPL:CAPE, MULTPL:SP500, IB:<symbol>, or a derived series: CAPE_PCT, NDX_ABOVE_200, SPX_ABOVE_200, NDX_DD, SPX_DD, SPX_LONG_DD, NDX_RSI, CPI_YOY, M2_YOY, ICSA_4W.
